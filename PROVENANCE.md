# Provenance and recovery debt

This subject was promoted out of the mixed public repository `jaredwilder/relation-family-atlas` on 2026-09-13.

The source summary records the following audit statements:

- `f(a,b,c;n)` is the largest subset of `[n]` with no solution to `ax+by=cz` in distinct `x,y,z`;
- eleven complete solver-optimal sequences were recovered;
- the collection contains 839 exact values;
- ten of those eleven sequences were reported absent from OEIS by the source sweep;
- six of seven sampled tables were independently re-derived on `n=3..20`;
- the `x+3y=4z` sequence has value 20 for every `46<=n<=64` and value 21 at `n=65`;
- a preregistered plateau generalization was falsified by the `x+4y=5z` measurement.

The original full machine-readable atlas is not branch-visible in the source repository. Consequently this focused home currently preserves the recovered result surface and the source-recovery obligation, but does not claim to contain all 839 table entries.
